# ASGRAM Play listing

App name: ASGRAM

Short description: An unofficial Telegram app with native chats, channels, groups and a fresh Feed.

## Full description

ASGRAM is an unofficial Telegram app that brings your conversations and public-channel stories together in a native Android experience.

Chat with your existing Telegram account. Explore private conversations, groups, channels and bots, with familiar Telegram messaging tools and settings.

Find a fresh perspective in Feed: browse recent public-channel posts, explore topics, read full stories, open photo albums and save stories on your device for later. Latest puts newer posts first, while For you offers another way to browse the available stories.

Use Spaces to find private chats, groups, channels and bots. Start a conversation or create a group or channel with native Telegram controls.

Choose a light or dark appearance using Telegram’s native theme settings. Your existing conversations stay on Telegram’s network.

ASGRAM is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Telegram. A Telegram account and an internet connection are required. Telegram controls account verification and login-code availability. Ordinary cloud chats are not end-to-end encrypted; Telegram Secret Chats are device-specific.

Store screenshots use English demo content. No real personal messages or account details are shown.
