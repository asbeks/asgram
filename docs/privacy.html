ASGRAM

Privacy policy

Updated October 29, 2026

ASGRAM is an unofficial Telegram app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Telegram. This policy describes ASGRAM’s Android client.

Your Telegram account

ASGRAM connects directly to Telegram’s services using Telegram’s API. Your phone number, profile, cloud messages, contacts you choose to synchronize, shared media, and optional location information are handled by Telegram according to Telegram’s privacy policy. Ordinary cloud chats are not end-to-end encrypted. Telegram Secret Chats use end-to-end encryption and are device-specific.

What ASGRAM adds

The Feed reads public Telegram channels. Saved stories, feed preferences, downloaded media, and cached Telegram account data are stored on your device. ASGRAM does not send private chats or your contact list to an ASGRAM-operated server. Featured channels can be shown without joining them. Joining a channel requires your action.

Optional permissions

Camera, microphone, photos, videos, files, contacts, notifications, Bluetooth and location access support features you choose, such as sending a photo, making a call, synchronizing contacts, receiving alerts or sharing your location. You can manage permissions in Android Settings. Denying a permission can limit its related feature.

Notifications and other providers

Google Firebase Cloud Messaging may process a device registration token and technical delivery information to deliver notifications. Analytics and advertising-ID collection are disabled in ASGRAM’s Firebase configuration. Optional native features can communicate with their service providers when used. Telegram controls delivery, anti-abuse measures and account verification; ASGRAM cannot guarantee login-code delivery.

Retention, export and deletion

You can clear local cache or sign out in the app, and remove local app data through Android Settings. These actions do not delete your Telegram account or cloud messages. To delete your Telegram account, use Telegram’s account deletion controls, including Telegram’s official account deletion page. Account deletion is permanent and is governed by Telegram.

Security and children

Use Telegram’s two-step verification, review active sessions and protect your device. No service can promise absolute security. ASGRAM is not directed to children; users must satisfy Telegram’s applicable minimum age and local requirements.

Contact

For ASGRAM privacy or support requests, contact support@fulbek.com or Telegram support. We may update this policy when the app changes.
