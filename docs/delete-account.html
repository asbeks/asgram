ASGRAM

Delete your account or data

ASGRAM is an unofficial Telegram client. Your account is a Telegram account, not a separate ASGRAM account.

Delete your Telegram account

  1. Open Telegram’s official account deletion page.
  2. Enter your Telegram phone number and follow Telegram’s confirmation steps.
  3. Review the warning and confirm only if you want to permanently delete your Telegram account across all clients.

This deletes the Telegram account and its associated cloud data according to Telegram’s deletion policy. Copies held by message recipients may remain. Any exceptions or retention required by Telegram are governed by that policy. ASGRAM does not keep a separate copy of your private chats on an ASGRAM server.

Remove ASGRAM from your device

To stop using ASGRAM without deleting Telegram, sign out in ASGRAM’s settings and use Android Settings → Apps → ASGRAM → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall ASGRAM. This removes ASGRAM’s local account data, preferences and caches. It does not delete your Telegram cloud account.

Delete selected data

Use the chat menu to delete selected messages, and Settings → Data and Storage → Storage Usage to clear downloaded media and caches. Telegram’s available deletion options depend on the chat and message.
